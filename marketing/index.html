Why Have Another Cherry

Built for the way you actually share money

Most splitters assume everyone pays equally. Real households don't. Cherry starts from a split that feels fair — then keeps the running math out of your way.

Splits that match income

Set any percentage between members — 50/50, 65/35, whatever's fair. Every expense divides automatically, no calculator required.

Snap a receipt, done

Point your camera at a receipt and AI reads the merchant, total, and category — then drops it into your shared ledger.

Settle without the spreadsheet

See exactly who owes whom at any moment. Record a payment, confirm it, and the balance zeroes out — cleanly.
