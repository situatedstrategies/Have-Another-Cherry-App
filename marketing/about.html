Our story

Splitting money shouldn't strain a relationship

Have Another Cherry started with a simple frustration: every expense app assumes everyone pays equally. But households don't work that way. People earn differently, contribute differently, and "fair" rarely means "50/50." We built the tool we wished existed — one that starts from a split that actually feels fair, then quietly handles the math so money never becomes the hard conversation.
