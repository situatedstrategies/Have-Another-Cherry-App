Privacy & Security

Your money stays your business

Have Another Cherry handles some of the most personal data there is — what you spend and who you share it with. We treat it that way. Here's exactly how your information is protected, and the choices you have over it.

What we collect

We collect only what's needed to run a shared expense splitter:

  • Account details — your name and email, provided when you sign in with Google or create an account.
  • Group & expense data — the household you create, the members you invite, your chosen split, and the expenses, settlements, and notes you log.
  • Optional profile data — income context and financial-profile quiz results, only if you choose to add them.
  • Receipt images — photos you upload for AI scanning, used to extract the amount, date, and category.

We don't ask for bank logins or card numbers, and we don't move money — you settle up directly with each other however you like.

How we use it

  • To calculate fair splits and keep every balance accurate.
  • To sync your group's expenses across members in real time.
  • To send transactional emails, such as invites and settle-up confirmations.
  • To power optional features you turn on, like receipt scanning and insights.

We do not sell your personal data, and we don't use your expense history to serve ads.

How we protect it

Encryption in transit & at rest Access-controlled database
  • Encrypted in transit. All traffic between your device and our servers is protected with HTTPS/TLS.
  • Encrypted at rest. Data is stored on Google Cloud (Firebase Firestore), which encrypts data at rest by default. Sensitive expense fields are additionally encrypted by the app before they're saved.
  • Strict access rules. Database security rules ensure only members of a group can read or write that group's data — no one else, including other users of the app.
  • Trusted infrastructure. The app runs on Google Cloud with secrets stored in a managed secret manager, never in code.

AI & receipts

Receipt scanning uses Google's Gemini models through Vertex AI to read the text on a receipt you upload. Images are sent securely to Google Cloud for processing so we can extract the merchant, total, date, and category. We use this data only to create your expense — not to train public models. If you never use receipt scanning, no receipt images are collected.

Who can see your data

  • Your group members. The people you invite to a household can see that household's shared expenses, splits, and settlements. That's the whole point.
  • Service providers. We rely on a small set of trusted processors to operate: Google Cloud / Firebase (hosting, authentication, database, AI) and Resend (transactional email). They process data on our behalf under their own security commitments.
  • No one else. We don't share or sell your data to advertisers or data brokers.

Your rights & controls

  • Access & export. You can view your group's full history in the app and download a backup at any time.
  • Correction. Edit or delete any expense, settlement, or profile detail directly.
  • Deletion. Ask us to delete your account and associated personal data by emailing the address below.

Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active so your household's history stays intact. If you delete your account, we remove your personal data from our active systems, except where we're required to retain limited records to comply with legal obligations.

Contact us

Questions about privacy or security, or a request to access or delete your data? Email hello@haveanothercherry.com and we'll get back to you.

This page describes our current practices in plain language. It isn't legal advice; please have your final privacy policy and terms reviewed by counsel before launch.
